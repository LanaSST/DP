Once upon a time there lived an old man and an old woman. The old man said to the old woman:
"Go on, old woman, scratch the box, sweep the bin, maybe you'll scrape up enough flour for a bun."
The old woman took a wing, scratched the box, swept the bin, and scraped up about two handfuls of flour. She kneaded the flour with sour cream, made a bun, fried it in butter, and put it on the windowsill to cool.
The bun lay there and lay there, then suddenly rolled off — from the window to the bench, from the bench to the floor, across the floor to the door, jumped over the threshold — into the entryway, from the entryway onto the porch, from the porch into the yard, from the yard through the gate, and on and on.
The bun rolls along the road, and toward him comes a Hare:
"Bun, Bun, I'm going to eat you!"
"Don't eat me, Hare, I'll sing you a song:
I'm a Bun, a Bun,
Scraped from the box,
Swept from the bin,
Kneaded with sour cream,
Fried in butter,
Cooled on the windowsill.
I ran away from Grandpa,
I ran away from Grandma,
And from you, Hare, I'll surely run away!"
And he rolled off down the road — the Hare barely saw him!
The bun rolls on, and toward him comes a Wolf:
"Bun, Bun, I'm going to eat you!"
"Don't eat me, Grey Wolf, I'll sing you a song:
I'm a Bun, a Bun,
Scraped from the box,
Swept from the bin,
Kneaded with sour cream,
Fried in butter,
Cooled on the windowsill.
I ran away from Grandpa,
I ran away from Grandma,
I ran away from the Hare,
And from you, Wolf, I'll surely run away!"
And he rolled off down the road — the Wolf barely saw him!
The bun rolls on, and toward him comes a Bear:
"Bun, Bun, I'm going to eat you!"
"Where would you, clumsy one, eat me!
I'm a Bun, a Bun,
Scraped from the box,
Swept from the bin,
Kneaded with sour cream,
Fried in butter,
Cooled on the windowsill.
I ran away from Grandpa,
I ran away from Grandma,
I ran away from the Hare,
I ran away from the Wolf,
And from you, Bear, I'll surely run away!"
And he rolled off again — the Bear barely saw him!
The bun rolls on, and toward him comes a Fox:
"Bun, Bun, where are you rolling?"
"I'm rolling along the path."
"Bun, Bun, sing me a song!"
And the Bun sang:
I'm a Bun, a Bun,
Scraped from the box,
Swept from the bin,
Kneaded with sour cream,
Fried in butter,
Cooled on the windowsill.
I ran away from Grandpa,
I ran away from Grandma,
I ran away from the Hare,
I ran away from the Wolf,
I ran away from the Bear,
And from you, Fox, it's no trick to run away!
And the Fox said:
"Ah, the song is lovely, but I hear poorly. Bun, Bun, sit on my nose and sing it once more, louder."
The Bun jumped onto the Fox's nose and sang the same song louder.
And the Fox said to him again:
"Bun, Bun, sit on my tongue and sing it one last time..."
