Once upon a time there lived a grandfather and a grandmother. They had a granddaughter called Mashenka.

One day her friends gathered to go into the forest — to pick mushrooms and berries. They came to invite Mashenka along too.

"Grandfather, grandmother," said Mashenka, "let me go into the forest with my friends!"

The grandfather and grandmother answered:

"Go, but mind you don't fall behind your friends — or you'll get lost."

The girls came to the forest and began gathering mushrooms and berries. And Mashenka — one tree after another, one bush after another — wandered far, far away from her friends.

She began calling out, began calling them. But her friends didn't hear, didn't answer.

Mashenka walked and walked through the forest — and got completely lost.

She came to the deepest, thickest part of the woods. She saw a little hut standing there. Mashenka knocked on the door — no answer. She pushed the door, and the door opened...
