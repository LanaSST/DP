One little girl left home and went into the forest. She got lost in the forest and began looking for the way home, but couldn't find it, and came upon a little house in the forest.

The door was open; she looked in the doorway and saw that there was no one in the house, and she went in. In this house there lived three bears. One bear was the father, and his name was Mikhailo Ivanovich. He was big and shaggy. The second was a mother bear. She was smaller, and her name was Nastasya Petrovna. The third was a little bear cub, and his name was Mishutka. The bears were not at home; they had gone for a walk in the forest.

The house had two rooms: one was the dining room, the other the bedroom. The girl went into the dining room and saw three cups of porridge on the table. The first cup, a very big one, belonged to Mikhailo Ivanovich. The second cup, a smaller one, belonged to Nastasya Petrovna; the third, a little blue cup, belonged to Mishutka. Beside each cup lay a spoon: a big one, a medium one, and a small one.

The girl took the biggest spoon and sipped from the biggest cup; then she took the medium spoon and sipped from the medium cup; then she took the little spoon and sipped from the little blue cup; and Mishutka's porridge seemed to her the best of all.

The girl wanted to sit down and saw three chairs at the table: one big one — Mikhailo Ivanovich's; another, smaller one — Nastasya Petrovna's; and the third, a little one with a little blue cushion — Mishutka's. She climbed onto the big chair and fell off; then she sat on the middle chair, but it was uncomfortable; then she sat on the little chair and laughed — it was so nice.

She took the little blue cup on her lap and began to eat. She ate all the porridge and began to rock on the chair.

The little chair broke, and she fell to the floor.

She got up, picked up the little chair, and went into the other room. There stood three beds: one big one — Mikhailo Ivanovich's; another, medium one — Nastasya Petrovna's; and the third, a little one — Mishenka's.

The girl lay down in the big one — it was too roomy for her; she lay down in the middle one — it was too high; she lay down in the little one — the little bed was just right for her, and she fell asleep.

And the bears came home hungry and wanted to have dinner.

The big bear took his cup, looked at it, and roared in a terrible voice:

"WHO HAS BEEN EATING FROM MY CUP?"

Nastasya Petrovna looked at her cup and growled, not so loudly:

"WHO HAS BEEN EATING FROM MY CUP?"

And Mishutka saw his empty little cup and squeaked in a thin voice:

"WHO HAS BEEN EATING FROM MY CUP AND EATEN IT ALL UP?"

Mikhailo Ivanovich looked at his chair and roared in a terrible voice:

"WHO HAS BEEN SITTING ON MY CHAIR AND MOVED IT FROM ITS PLACE?"

Nastasya Petrovna looked at her chair and growled, not so loudly:

"WHO HAS BEEN SITTING ON MY CHAIR AND MOVED IT FROM ITS PLACE?"

Mishutka looked at his broken little chair and squeaked:

"WHO HAS BEEN SITTING ON MY CHAIR AND BROKEN IT?.."
