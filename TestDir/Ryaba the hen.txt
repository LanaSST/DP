Here's the English translation:

Once upon a time there lived a grandfather and a grandmother.
They had a hen called Ryaba.
The hen laid an egg, and not just an ordinary one — a golden one.
The grandfather beat it, beat it — couldn't break it.
The grandmother beat it, beat it — couldn't break it.
A mouse ran by, flicked its tail, the egg fell and broke.
The grandfather cries, the grandmother cries, and the hen clucks:
"Don't cry, grandfather, don't cry, grandmother: I'll lay you a new egg — not a golden one, but an ordinary one!"
